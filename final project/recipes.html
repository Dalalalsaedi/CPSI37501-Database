Fluffy Pancakes

Cuisine: American

Prep Time: 12 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 2 Eggs

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, milk, and eggs together.
  2. Pour the batter onto a heated pan.
  3. Cook until golden brown on both sides.

Chicken Alfredo

Cuisine: Italian

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Chicken
  • 2 cups Pasta
  • 1 cup Alfredo Sauce

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta according to package directions.
  2. Cook the chicken until fully done.
  3. Mix the pasta, chicken, and Alfredo sauce together.
